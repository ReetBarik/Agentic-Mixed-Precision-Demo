  per integral, target `kokkosUtils.h:212`).
* `flows_to_scored`: the journal is absent (whole-app journal empty, as known),
  so this is the **coarse cut** — direct source read of the region bodies +
  `res(i,*)` assignments in the per-integral trees, confirming every cell sits in
  the special-function / coefficient-computation path. No cell is off the scored
  surface, so `flows_to_scored=true` for all 31 and there are **no
  `scoring_invisible` cells**.

**Load-bearing physics (read from the trees):** the scored coefficients are
`res(i,0/1/2)`. The finite part is a dilog cancellation combination, e.g.
`B1m.h:242 res(i,0) = dilog4 - dilog5 - 2*dilog1 - ...` (B10) and
`B2m.h:241 res(i,0) = ... - 2*dilog1 - dilog2 - dilog3` (B12). The `dilogN` are
computed **in double** by `ql::Li2omrat / Li2omx2 → ql::ddilog` (the Chebyshev
dilog in `kokkosUtils.h`, whose branches are lines 174/183/199/206/212/703).
The catastrophic cancellation is the subtraction of near-equal double dilogs at
the `res(i,0)` line. Widening any **single** link of this chain to dd is inert:
the digits are already lost in the double dilog inputs before the combination,
and widening only the combination line cannot recover them. This is directly
confirmed by measurement — `kokkosUtils.h:212` (the dilog itself) is measured
byte-identical (INERT) in all four integrals that have dd cells.

**The single cell worth naming for Reet — #18, B12 `B2m.h:241`.** This is the one

already demonstrates empirically. Recovering B12's floor would require widening
the **entire** chain (dilog-arguments → dilogs → `res(i,0)` combination) to dd
simultaneously — a whole-chain / whole-integral dd promotion, effectively the dd
oracle — or an algorithmic rewrite of the cancellation. Both are out of scope
for this arc (Reet: no algorithmic rewrites; the measurement layer promotes
single regions). So #18 is `static_inert` in practice and not a single-region
gen gap. It is the strongest "maybe," and it still resolves to No.

## Why every classification collapses to inert (the three legs)

1. **Measured proof (7 cells).** `kokkosUtils.h:212` — the dilog itself, and for
   B10 the *documented* whole-app floor region — is byte-identical under dd in
   B10/B8/B9/B12. Three more stable dilog branches in B8 (174/199/206) likewise.
   Direct evidence that single-region dd on both `log_near_root` and `stable`
   dilog regions is inert in this codebase.

2. **Static proof (24 cells).** `write_truncation` (18) and `promotion_no_op`
   (6) are the validated 2d-B / 2c inertness gates (they were tuned to spare real
   DISCRIM cells, e.g. `boxGPU.h:139` ff — see the Phase 2c/2d records). A cell
   that trips them has either a double-typed write boundary that truncates the
   widening, or an empty promotable payload. Both are structural no-ops
   regardless of signal_class or hotspot status.

3. **Physics (why 1+2 are not artifacts).** The scored floor is the subtraction
   of near-equal **double** dilogs at `res(i,0)`. dd cannot recover digits
   destroyed before the subtraction; it would have to be applied across the whole
   dilog→combination chain at once. Single-region promotion — the only kind the
   fan-out/solver measurement layer produces — cannot span that chain, which is
   exactly why legs 1 and 2 both come back inert.
